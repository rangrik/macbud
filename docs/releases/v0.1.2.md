# MacBud v0.1.2

The notch now lives on every display, and the island opens on the one you are working on.

- Every attached display gets its own notch: the real one on a MacBook, a drawn one on external monitors. Each keeps its own geometry, so the tab and toasts sit correctly on screens of any size.
- The shortcut opens the island on the display you are active on. MacBud asks accessibility for the focused window first, falls back to the pointer, then to the menu-bar screen, and gives up after 0.2 s so a hung app can never stall the hot key.
- Plugging in, unplugging, or rearranging displays rebuilds the notches in place; no restart needed.
- Settings → Behaviour renames the toggle to "Show a clickable notch tab on every display". Turn it off to remove the drawn notch from external monitors.

Requires macOS 26 or later. Universal (Apple Silicon + Intel). Signed with an Apple Development certificate and **not notarized**, so macOS may block a downloaded copy.
