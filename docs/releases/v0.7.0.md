# MacBud v0.7.0

⌥Space now opens on what you most likely want, instead of wherever you left off, and learns from where you actually go.

- A screenshot or dictation from the last minute opens straight on it; otherwise the notch opens on Clipboard.
- With Codex installed, a small model (gpt-6-luna) predicts in the background from metadata only: which app is in front, what kind of thing you copied and how long ago, the time of day. It never sees what you copied, dictated or saved.
- When its guesses miss, a bigger model (gpt-6-sol) reads the misses and rewrites the strategies the small model follows.
- Opening never waits for a model, and anything that goes wrong falls back to the simple rules.
- Settings → Prediction shows hit rates for the model and the rules, calls and tokens today, the current strategies, your recent opens, and the exact prompt and reply of every call. There is a daily call cap, a switch to turn Codex off, and Reset memory.
- It runs on your existing Codex login and saves nothing to your Codex history.

Requires macOS 26 or later. Universal (Apple Silicon + Intel). Signed with an Apple Development
certificate and **not notarized**, so macOS may block a downloaded copy.
