# MacBud v0.8.3

Search everything now finds your open windows and apps too, and the shelf can offer the window you most likely want next.

- Type under All and matching windows and apps show up next to your copies, screenshots, dictations and snippets, with the app's icon, the window's title and the app's name. ↩ switches to the window or opens the app, and the preview shows the window.
- With nothing typed, All still shows your recent items. The Apps filter works as before.
- When prediction expects you to switch apps, the shelf puts that window or app first with the ring on it. ↩ or a click switches to it. When it cannot tell which one, it opens the Apps filter as before.
- Prediction learns which app you switch to from app identifiers only, never window titles.

Requires macOS 26 or later. Universal (Apple Silicon + Intel). Signed with an Apple Development
certificate and **not notarized**, so macOS may block a downloaded copy.
