# MacBud v0.6.2

The MacBud logo on the notch now tells you whether Keep Alive is on.

- The logo is in color while Keep Alive keeps your Mac awake, and plain white while it is off, like the menu bar icon.
- The sun and pause icons are gone from the closed notch, so the right side shows just the logo.

Requires macOS 26 or later. Universal (Apple Silicon + Intel). Signed with an Apple Development
certificate and **not notarized**, so macOS may block a downloaded copy.
