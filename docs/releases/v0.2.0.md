# MacBud v0.2.0

Pause while you are dictating, click a word MacBud got wrong, and fix it — then carry on talking.

- Words the recogniser was unsure of are underlined while you speak, so you spot a mistake straight away.
- Clicking a word pauses the microphone and offers what it nearly heard instead; pick one or type your own, press Return, and keep going. Your fix stays put as new words arrive.
- Escape now backs out of the word you are fixing. Press it again to cancel the recording.
- Remove a word you never said without stopping the recording.
- Every correction teaches MacBud the word. It tells the recogniser about it before your next recording, and once you have fixed the same word twice it also replaces it in finished text.
- Settings › Dictation lists the words you have taught it, and lets you forget any of them.
- Pointing at the transcript shrinks the level meter so more of your words fit.

Requires macOS 26 or later. Universal (Apple Silicon + Intel). Signed with an Apple Development
certificate and **not notarized**, so macOS may block a downloaded copy.
