# MacBud v0.8.0

The notch is now a shelf: rest the pointer on it, click it or press your shortcut, and your six newest things are right there, ready to paste.

- Copied text, links, images and files, screenshots, and dictations sit together in one row, newest on the left. ← → pick one, ↩ copies or pastes it, a click does the same.
- Resting the pointer on the notch opens the shelf without taking the keyboard from your app, and moving away closes it. Hold your shortcut to peek and let go to close. Turn hovering off in Settings → General.
- Press ↓, click Search everything, or just start typing to search all of it at once. The five tabs are gone; filters narrow the list instead: All, Text, Links, Images, Screenshots, Dictations, Snippets, Apps. ⇥ cycles them and ⌘1–⌘8 jump to one, always in the same order.
- The full view keeps what you had: a preview of the selected item, pin, delete, Quick Look, Finder, save as snippet, the snippet editor, and the Apps filter with your open windows.
- Prediction now puts the ring on the item you most likely want, and opens the Apps filter when you most likely want another app.
- Your shortcuts carry over: a shortcut that opened a tab now opens its filter (Clipboard opens Search everything).

Requires macOS 26 or later. Universal (Apple Silicon + Intel). Signed with an Apple Development
certificate and **not notarized**, so macOS may block a downloaded copy.
