# MacBud v0.8.1

You can now read everything the prediction models do, in one window that is easy to find.

- Open **Prediction Activity** from the menu bar, from the button at the top of Settings → Prediction, or with ⌘L while MacBud is open. The window remembers its size and place.
- A live timeline, newest first, shows every model call, every open with what MacBud predicted, whether it was a hit or a miss, each review, and any errors. New calls appear as they happen.
- Click a row to see exactly what was sent and what came back, the moment the model saw, its note, and what you used next. Every part has a Copy button.
- A review shows the strategies before and after, with added lines in green and removed lines in red.
- Filter by Driver, Reviewer, Opens, Misses or Errors, search the prompts and replies, and export what you see to a Markdown file.
- The top of the window shows today's calls and tokens, the 7-day hit rates of the model and of the rules, and the current strategies, with Review now and Open memory folder.
- Settings → Prediction now holds only the switches and the numbers, so nothing is shown twice.

Requires macOS 26 or later. Universal (Apple Silicon + Intel). Signed with an Apple Development
certificate and **not notarized**, so macOS may block a downloaded copy.
