# MacBud v0.8.5

The shelf opens on the display under your pointer, even when your active app is on another screen.

- Open the shelf on the screen you are pointing at, including when the pointer rests on its top menu-bar row.
- Start dictation on the pointer’s screen, including when the shelf is already open elsewhere.
- Keep opening a screen’s own shelf by clicking or hovering its notch tab.

Requires macOS 26 or later. Universal (Apple Silicon + Intel). Signed with an Apple Development
certificate and **not notarized**, so macOS may block a downloaded copy.
